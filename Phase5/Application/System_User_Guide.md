# Yedidim Organization Management System
---
# Introduction

Welcome to the Volunteer and Service Call Management System.

This system is designed to provide centralized management of volunteers, service calls, training programs, availability schedules, and additional operational data.

The system offers a user-friendly and intuitive interface that enables administrators to perform all required operations quickly, efficiently, and easily.

---

# Main Menu

The main menu provides access to the following screens:

* Call Management
* Open Calls
* General History
* Volunteer Management
* Training Management
* Volunteer Availability
* Volunteer Assignment
* Smart Actions Wizard

---

# Volunteer Management

This screen allows users to view and manage all volunteers in the system.

## Adding a New Volunteer

1. Click the **"New Volunteer"** button.
2. Fill in the volunteer's details.
3. Select the required skills and availability.
4. Click **"Save"**.

## Editing a Volunteer

1. Click the pencil icon next to the volunteer.
2. Update the desired information.
3. Click **"Save"**.

## Deleting a Volunteer

1. Click the trash can icon.
2. Confirm the deletion.

---

# Call Management

This screen displays all service calls in the system.

## Creating a New Call

1. Click **"New Call"**.
2. Enter the call details:

   * Call description
   * Address or location
   * Call type
   * Priority level
   * Caller information
3. Click **"Save"**.

## Editing a Call

1. Click the pencil icon.
2. Update the required information.
3. Click **"Save"**.

## Deleting a Call

1. Click the trash can icon.
2. Confirm the deletion.

## Closing a Call

Once the call has been handled, it can be marked as completed and moved to the history section.

---

# Open Calls

This screen displays all active calls that have not yet been closed.

The screen allows users to:

* View active calls.
* Track call status.
* Assign volunteers to calls.
* Update the call status.

---

# General History

The history screen displays all previously completed calls.

It is used for:

* Monitoring and supervision.
* Information retrieval and data analysis.
* Organizational activity documentation.

---

# Training Management

This screen provides full management of the organization's training programs.

## Adding a Training Program

1. Click **"New Training"**.
2. Enter the training details:

   * Training name
   * Training description
   * Training duration
   * Maximum number of participants
3. Click **"Save Training"**.

## Editing a Training Program

1. Click the pencil icon.
2. Update the information.
3. Click **"Save"**.

## Deleting a Training Program

1. Click the trash can icon.
2. Confirm the deletion.

---

# Volunteer Availability

This screen displays volunteers' activity schedules and availability.

Users can:

* View volunteer availability.
* Update working hours.
* Manage shifts and volunteering schedules.

---

# Volunteer Assignment

This screen allows matching volunteers with service calls.

The system assists administrators in identifying suitable volunteers and performing efficient assignments according to the requirements of each call.

---

# Smart Actions Wizard

This screen provides automatic tools that assist in decision-making.

## Finding the Outstanding Volunteer

The system displays the volunteer who handled the highest number of service calls.

## Finding Calls Without Assigned Volunteers

The system displays a list of open calls that have not yet been assigned to a volunteer.

---

# Usage Recommendations

* Update call statuses regularly.
* Keep volunteer information up to date.
* Update availability schedules periodically.
* Perform periodic reviews using the history and reports screens.
* Use the Smart Actions Wizard to support quick decision-making.

---

# Summary

The Volunteer and Service Call Management System centralizes all the information required to manage the organization in a single platform.

The system enables efficient workforce management, improves operational processes, and provides faster and higher-quality responses to service requests and calls.
